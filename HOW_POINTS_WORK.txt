CHOREWARS: HOW THE POINTS WORK
==============================

THE BASICS
----------
Each area (Kitchen, Hall, Sink, Bathrooms) has its own rotation of people.

1. When an area looks dirty, anyone can tap "Needs cleaning".
   Flagging gives no points, it just tells the next person it's their turn.
2. The person whose turn it is cleans it and taps "Mark as done".
3. The turn then moves to the next person in that area's rotation.

If the area actually looks fine, the person whose turn it is can start a
"Looks fine" vote. If half the people on that rotation agree, the flag is
cleared and nobody gains or loses anything.


EARNING POINTS
--------------
  Cleaning on your turn ............ +10
  Rescuing someone else's turn ..... +12
  Early bonus ...................... +2   (cleaned before the usual gap was up)
  Streak bonus ..................... +3 on your 3rd clean in a row
                                     +5 on your 5th
                                     +10 on your 10th

Your streak counts every turn you do yourself, across all areas.
It only resets if someone has to rescue one of your turns.


RESCUES (VOLUNTEERING)
----------------------
If a flagged area hasn't been cleaned within 24 hours, anyone else can
volunteer to do it.

  - The person who rescues it gets +12.
  - The person whose turn it was loses 5 points and their streak resets.
  - Even after someone volunteers, the person whose turn it is can still
    clean it themselves. Then it counts as a normal clean for them and the
    volunteer gets nothing.


THE LEAGUE: ROUNDS, NOT MONTHS
------------------------------
We don't compete by calendar month any more, because in one month someone
might get 3 turns and someone else none. That isn't fair.

Instead, the league is played in ROUNDS:

  - A round ends once everyone has had 3 turns.
    A turn counts once the area gets cleaned, by you or by someone who
    rescued you.
  - If a round is taking too long, it ends anyway after 45 days.

Only your FIRST 3 TURNS in a round count toward your score. So getting
more turns than others doesn't give you an advantage.

Rescues you do for other people ALWAYS count, so volunteering is the way
to climb the table.


END OF A ROUND
--------------
  WINNER (highest score)
    Gets a Skip Pass and the League Champion badge, and wears the crown
    during the next round.

  LOWEST SCORER
    Does the forfeit the house has agreed on (for example, buying snacks
    for everyone). They tick it off in the app once it's done.

The forfeit is only given to people who actually had all 3 of their turns.
If you joined partway through a round or were away, you can't get it.
If fewer than two people had all their turns before the 45 days ran out,
there is no forfeit that round.


SKIP PASS
---------
The round winner gets a Skip Pass. When it's your turn on a flagged area,
you can use it to pass that task on:

  - The task goes to the person who came LAST in the previous round.
  - If they're away, it goes to the next person in the rotation instead.
  - It counts as their turn, not yours. You get no points, but no penalty,
    and your streak stays safe.
  - A task that was passed to you with a Skip Pass can't be skipped again.
  - Once it's cleaned, the rotation carries on as if you had done it.


AWAY
----
If you're travelling, ask an admin to mark you as away.
While you're away:
  - You're skipped in every rotation.
  - You can't be penalised.
  - The round doesn't wait for you, and you can't get the forfeit.


WEEKLY CHAMPION
---------------
Separately, whoever scores the most points each week is crowned Weekly
Champion. It's just for bragging rights. All-time points are kept forever.


Note: the numbers above are the defaults. An admin can change them in the
app's settings.
